# What is e-Assisted Climb?

e-Assisted Climb is the electronic version of The Assisted Climb — a structured learning platform designed for schools. It gives students a guided, step-by-step workspace to research, think, and write, with an AI tutor that helps them discover answers for themselves rather than simply giving them the answer.

---

## For Teachers

e-Assisted Climb puts you in control. You set the task, the question, the vocabulary you want students to use, and the success criteria you want them to work towards. The platform then guides every student through the same structured stages — at their own pace, with AI support — while you watch their progress in real time.

You can see exactly what every student has written at every stage, read the full conversation they had with the AI tutor, and leave your own feedback. When marking, the AI can draft comments and grades for you to review and edit before anything is saved — so it speeds up your workload without replacing your professional judgement.

At a class level, you get an instant picture of where the whole group is struggling, what misconceptions came up most, and a suggested plan for your next lesson based on what actually happened in this one.

---

## For Students

e-Assisted Climb gives you a structured space to think through a topic properly — not just look up an answer and copy it.

You work through a series of stages:

- **MAP** — you start by writing down what you already know and what questions you have.
- **TORCH** — you have a conversation with an AI tutor that asks you questions, challenges your thinking, and helps you build your understanding. It won't write the answer for you — it helps you find it yourself.
- **RESEARCH** — you find and evaluate your own sources.
- **COMPASS** — you check your thinking, spot any weaknesses, and decide whether you're ready to write.
- **Final Response** — you write your finished answer.

Your teacher can see your work as you go and leave feedback to help you improve before you finish.

---

## For Parents

e-Assisted Climb is a tool your child uses in school (and sometimes at home) to help them think independently about a topic set by their teacher.

Rather than searching for an answer and copying it, your child is guided through a structured process — recording what they already know, having a conversation with a carefully designed AI tutor, researching sources, checking their own reasoning, and writing a final response.

The AI tutor is designed to ask questions and encourage thinking, not to do the work for your child. Everything your child writes is visible to their teacher, who reviews their progress and leaves personal feedback.

The school controls all the settings, including which AI the platform uses. No personal data is shared outside the school's own system.
